# Divine Mercy Chaplet

## Overview
The Divine Mercy Chaplet is a Catholic devotion associated with the message of Divine Mercy and commonly prayed using Rosary beads. It is a private devotion, not a replacement for the Mass or the Sacrament of Reconciliation.

## Common structure
The commonly used form includes:
1. The Sign of the Cross.
2. Opening prayers, often including the Our Father, Hail Mary, and Apostles' Creed.
3. On the large beads, the prayer: “Eternal Father, I offer you the Body and Blood, Soul and Divinity of your dearly beloved Son, our Lord Jesus Christ, in atonement for our sins and those of the whole world.”
4. On the small beads, the response: “For the sake of his sorrowful Passion, have mercy on us and on the whole world.”
5. Repeat for five decades.
6. Conclude with the customary closing invocation, repeated three times.

Use an approved prayer book or reliable Catholic source for the complete exact text and any local language version.

## Guidance for Grace
Explain the devotion respectfully. Do not claim that praying it mechanically guarantees a desired result. Encourage trust in God's mercy, conversion, compassion, and works of mercy.
