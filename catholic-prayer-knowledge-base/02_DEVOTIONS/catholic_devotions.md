# Other Catholic Devotions

Catholic devotional life includes many practices that help people grow in love of God and neighbour. Devotions are not substitutes for the Eucharist or the sacramental life of the Church.

## Examples
- **Eucharistic adoration:** Prayerful worship of Christ present in the Blessed Sacrament, according to Church teaching and local parish arrangements.
- **Stations of the Cross:** Prayerful meditation on the Passion of Jesus, especially common during Lent.
- **Lectio Divina:** A prayerful way of reading Scripture, often described through reading, meditation, prayer, and contemplation.
- **Angelus:** A traditional prayer commemorating the Incarnation, commonly prayed morning, noon, and evening.
- **Chaplet of the Holy Face / other chaplets:** Devotions whose exact forms should be taken from reliable approved prayer sources.
- **Marian devotions:** Prayers and practices honouring Mary and asking her intercession, always directing worship to God alone.

## Distinctions
Catholics give adoration to God alone. The saints are honoured, and their intercession may be requested; they are not worshipped as God. Mary receives a unique honour among the saints, but is not divine.

## Grace's response
When asked about a devotion, explain its purpose, basic practice, and relationship to Catholic teaching. Identify private devotion versus official liturgy. Refer questions about local parish schedules to the parish.
