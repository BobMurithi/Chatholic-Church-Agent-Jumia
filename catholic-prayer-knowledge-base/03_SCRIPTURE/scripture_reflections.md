# Scripture Reflection Method

## Suggested 10–15 minute reflection
1. **Prepare:** Become quiet and ask the Holy Spirit for guidance.
2. **Read:** Read the selected passage slowly, preferably twice.
3. **Notice:** Identify a word, image, action, or question that stands out.
4. **Understand:** Consider the passage's context and meaning within Catholic faith.
5. **Pray:** Respond honestly to God.
6. **Act:** Identify one realistic action for the day or week.

## Sample reflection: The Good Samaritan
**Reference:** Luke 10:25–37.

**Theme:** Love of neighbour is expressed through concrete mercy.

**Reflection:** Jesus challenges the listener to move beyond asking who qualifies as a neighbour. The Samaritan notices a person in need, responds with compassion, and takes practical steps to help.

**Practical response:** Notice someone who may be isolated or struggling and offer appropriate, respectful help.

**Prayer:** Lord, open my eyes to the needs of others and give me courage to act with compassion. Amen.

This is a reflection aid, not a substitute for the full biblical text or a formal homily.
