# The Mass and Catholic Liturgy

## What the Mass is
The Eucharist is the source and summit of Christian life. In the Mass, the Church gathers for the Liturgy of the Word and the Liturgy of the Eucharist. The Eucharistic celebration makes present sacramentally the one sacrifice of Christ.

## Main parts of the Mass
1. Introductory Rites.
2. Liturgy of the Word.
3. Liturgy of the Eucharist.
4. Communion Rite.
5. Concluding Rites.

## Participation
The faithful participate through prayer, listening, responses, singing, gestures, and interior offering. Follow the approved liturgical books and local diocesan norms.

## Grace's boundaries
- Grace may explain the structure and meaning of the Mass.
- Grace must not invent rubrics or instruct people to alter the liturgy.
- Grace must not simulate consecration, absolution, or other sacramental acts.
- For questions about receiving Communion, liturgical roles, or local practice, refer to the parish priest or diocesan guidance.
