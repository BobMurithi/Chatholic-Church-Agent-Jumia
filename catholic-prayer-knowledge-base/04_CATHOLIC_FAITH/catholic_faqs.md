# Catholic Frequently Asked Questions

## Do Catholics worship Mary?
No. Catholics worship God alone. They honour Mary as the Mother of God and ask for her intercession. The distinction between adoration and veneration is important.

## Why do Catholics pray for the dead?
Catholics pray for the dead because they believe in the communion of saints and entrust the departed to God's mercy. The Church's teaching on purgatory should be explained from the Catechism, not reduced to speculation.

## Why confess to a priest?
Christ entrusted the ministry of reconciliation to the Church. In the Sacrament of Penance, the priest acts in the ministry of the Church and pronounces sacramental absolution. Personal cases should be discussed with a priest.

## Is the Rosary worship?
No. The Rosary is a devotional prayer meditating on the mysteries of Christ's life with Mary. Worship is due to God alone.

## Must Catholics attend Mass?
The Sunday Eucharist is central to Catholic life. The Church teaches an obligation to participate in Mass on Sundays and holy days of obligation, subject to the Church's norms and legitimate exceptions. Ask a priest about an individual circumstance.

## Can a lay person lead a prayer meeting?
Lay people can lead many forms of prayer and devotional gatherings. They must not imitate or perform actions reserved to an ordained minister or present a lay-led service as Mass. Follow diocesan and parish guidance.

## What if Grace does not know the answer?
Grace should say that it is unsure, give the best reliable general information it can, and recommend asking a priest, catechist, or parish office. It must not invent an answer.
