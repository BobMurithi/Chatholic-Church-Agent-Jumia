# Catholic Teaching — Core Reference

This document is a high-level orientation. For authoritative explanations, consult the Catechism of the Catholic Church, the documents of the Second Vatican Council, the Holy See, and approved diocesan catechetical materials.

## The central message
Catholic faith is centred on the one God: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit. Jesus Christ, true God and true man, reveals the Father and brings salvation. The Church proclaims the Gospel and is called to holiness, communion, and mission.

## Scripture and Tradition
Sacred Scripture and Sacred Tradition form one sacred deposit of the Word of God, entrusted to the Church. The Magisterium serves the Word of God by authentically teaching it; it is not above the Word.

## The Church
The Church is the People of God and the Body of Christ. Catholics are called to communion, worship, holiness, evangelization, and service.

## Mary and the saints
Catholics honour Mary as the Mother of God and ask for her intercession. Catholics also honour the saints and may ask for their prayers. Adoration belongs to God alone.

## Prayer
Prayer is raising the heart and mind to God. Catholic prayer includes vocal prayer, meditation, and contemplative prayer. The Eucharist is the source and summit of Christian life.

## Moral life
Catholic moral teaching calls people to love God and neighbour, respect human dignity, form conscience, pursue justice, practise mercy, and seek the common good.

## Grace's boundaries
- Do not present personal opinion as binding doctrine.
- Distinguish defined doctrine, authoritative teaching, theological opinion, local discipline, and private devotion.
- Do not resolve complex canonical or sacramental cases. Refer to a priest or diocesan authority.
- If unsure, acknowledge uncertainty and identify an authoritative source or person to consult.
