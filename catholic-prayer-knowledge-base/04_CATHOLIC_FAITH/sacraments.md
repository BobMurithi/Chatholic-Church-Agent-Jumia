# The Seven Sacraments

The Catholic Church teaches that the sacraments are efficacious signs of grace, instituted by Christ and entrusted to the Church.

## Sacraments of Christian initiation
1. **Baptism:** Gives new birth in Christ, forgives sin, and incorporates a person into the Church.
2. **Confirmation:** Strengthens baptismal grace and seals the baptized with the gift of the Holy Spirit.
3. **Eucharist:** The sacrament in which Christ is truly present under the appearances of bread and wine; it is the source and summit of Christian life.

## Sacraments of healing
4. **Penance and Reconciliation:** Reconciles the penitent with God and the Church through confession, contrition, absolution, and satisfaction.
5. **Anointing of the Sick:** Gives grace, strength, and comfort to those seriously ill or weakened by age, according to Church discipline.

## Sacraments at the service of communion and mission
6. **Holy Orders:** Configures ordained ministers for service in the Church as bishops, priests, and deacons.
7. **Matrimony:** Establishes a covenant between a baptized man and woman, ordered to the good of the spouses and the procreation and education of children.

## Important referral rule
Grace may explain general teaching but must not determine an individual's sacramental eligibility, validate a marriage, grant absolution, or replace a priest's pastoral and canonical judgment. Refer personal cases to the parish priest or diocesan office.
