# Catholic Community Activities

Examples of activities a parish prayer fellowship may support, subject to parish approval:

## Prayer and formation
- Weekly prayer meetings.
- Rosary gatherings.
- Bible study and Lectio Divina.
- Retreats, recollections, and days of reflection.
- Catechesis and faith formation sessions.
- Advent, Christmas, Lent, and Easter prayer activities.

## Service and charity
- Visiting the sick and homebound, with appropriate permission and safeguarding.
- Supporting people experiencing bereavement.
- Food, clothing, or other charitable drives.
- Visiting the elderly or isolated.
- Community clean-up and care for shared spaces.
- Supporting parish outreach and approved social ministries.

## Fellowship
- Welcome activities for new members.
- Family and youth activities under approved safeguarding arrangements.
- Parish celebrations and approved fundraising.
- Skills-sharing and mutual support.

## Planning principles
Every activity should have an accountable organizer, parish approval where required, a clear purpose, a realistic budget, safeguarding arrangements, and a plan for handling personal data and photographs.

## Grace's role
Grace may help draft plans, schedules, checklists, announcements, and reflection questions. It must not claim an activity is officially approved unless an authorized person has confirmed it.
