# Fellowship Guidelines

## Welcome and respect
Treat every participant with dignity. Welcome people regardless of their familiarity with Catholic prayer or their ability to speak English or Kiswahili.

## Sharing and listening
- Listen without ridicule or interruption.
- Do not pressure people to share private experiences.
- Avoid gossip and the public disclosure of another person's intention.
- Speak with charity, honesty, and humility.

## Prayer intentions
- Obtain permission before recording or sharing a person's name or personal circumstances.
- Offer an anonymous option.
- Do not publish sensitive details in public channels without explicit consent.
- Keep retention and deletion practices consistent with the fellowship's privacy policy.

## Safeguarding
Concerns involving abuse, exploitation, threats, or risk to a child or vulnerable adult must be directed promptly to the designated safeguarding lead and appropriate authorities in accordance with local law and parish policy. Do not investigate allegations through the AI assistant.

## Disagreement
Encourage respectful discussion. For disputes about doctrine, discipline, or pastoral decisions, refer participants to a priest or qualified catechist rather than escalating conflict.

## No coercion
Prayer, participation, donations, and sharing must not be coerced. Do not promise healing, financial gain, or guaranteed outcomes in exchange for prayer or donations.
