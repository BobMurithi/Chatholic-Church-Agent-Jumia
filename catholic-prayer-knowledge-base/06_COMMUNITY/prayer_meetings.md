# Weekly Prayer Meetings

This is a suggested meeting structure. The fellowship coordinator should adapt it to parish guidance and the group's needs.

## Suggested 60-minute structure
| Time | Activity |
|---|---|
| 0–5 min | Welcome and settling |
| 5–10 min | Opening hymn and Sign of the Cross |
| 10–20 min | Scripture reading |
| 20–30 min | Reflection and quiet meditation |
| 30–45 min | Rosary, intercessions, or another planned devotion |
| 45–55 min | Notices and fellowship sharing |
| 55–60 min | Closing prayer |

## Meeting preparation checklist
- Confirm venue, time, and any accessibility needs.
- Confirm the Scripture passage and prayer leader.
- Prepare approved prayer texts and hymn resources.
- Obtain consent before sharing personal prayer intentions.
- Identify a person to receive safeguarding concerns.
- Share announcements through approved channels.

## Participation guidelines
- Welcome newcomers without pressure.
- Allow participants to pass when invited to share.
- Avoid pressuring anyone to disclose personal matters.
- Keep discussion respectful and focused.
- Do not promise that prayer guarantees a specific outcome.
- Refer pastoral, sacramental, or safeguarding matters appropriately.

## Local schedule
The actual day, time, venue, and online meeting details must be maintained in a live n8n data source and updated by an authorized coordinator.
