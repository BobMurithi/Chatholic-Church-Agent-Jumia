# Traditional Catholic Prayers

This document describes common prayers and provides short reference forms. Before publishing prayer texts for communal or liturgical use, verify the exact wording against an approved Catholic prayer book or the local parish/diocese.

## Sign of the Cross
**English:** In the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit. Amen.

**Kiswahili (common form):** Kwa jina la Baba, na la Mwana, na la Roho Mtakatifu. Amina.

## Our Father / Baba Yetu
**English:** Our Father, who art in heaven, hallowed be thy name; thy kingdom come; thy will be done on earth as it is in heaven. Give us this day our daily bread; and forgive us our trespasses, as we forgive those who trespass against us; and lead us not into temptation, but deliver us from evil. Amen.

**Kiswahili:** Use the approved wording customary in the participant's parish or diocese. Do not silently substitute an unverified translation.

## Hail Mary / Salamu Maria
**English:** Hail Mary, full of grace, the Lord is with thee. Blessed art thou among women, and blessed is the fruit of thy womb, Jesus. Holy Mary, Mother of God, pray for us sinners, now and at the hour of our death. Amen.

**Kiswahili:** Use the approved wording customary in the participant's parish or diocese.

## Glory Be / Atukuzwe Baba
**English:** Glory be to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Spirit; as it was in the beginning, is now, and ever shall be, world without end. Amen.

**Kiswahili:** Use the approved wording customary in the participant's parish or diocese.

## Apostles' Creed
The Apostles' Creed is an ancient summary of Christian belief used in Catholic prayer. For the complete text, use the locally approved prayer-book wording. Grace may explain its themes: belief in God the Father, Jesus Christ, the Holy Spirit, the Church, forgiveness of sins, resurrection of the body, and life everlasting.

## Act of Contrition
An Act of Contrition expresses sorrow for sin and a sincere desire to turn back to God. There are approved and customary forms. Grace may explain the purpose, but should provide the locally approved text when asked for an exact formula.

## Prayer before meals
**English sample:** Bless us, O Lord, and these your gifts, which we are about to receive from your goodness, through Christ our Lord. Amen.

**Kiswahili sample:** Ee Bwana, utubariki sisi na chakula hiki tunachokipokea kwa wema wako. Kwa njia ya Kristo Bwana wetu. Amina.

This is a simple sample; confirm local customary wording.

## Prayer after meals
**English sample:** We give you thanks, almighty God, for all your benefits, who live and reign for ever and ever. Amen.

**Kiswahili sample:** Tunakushukuru, Ee Mungu Mwenyezi, kwa neema zako zote. Wewe unayeishi na kutawala milele na milele. Amina.

This is a sample translation, not a claim of official liturgical wording.

## Guidance for Grace
- Ask whether the participant wants the prayer in English or Kiswahili if language is unclear.
- Preserve the wording of established prayers; do not improvise or alter them while presenting them as official.
- If the exact approved Kiswahili version is unavailable, say so and offer an explanation or a clearly labelled sample translation.
- Do not present a private devotional prayer as part of the official Mass.
