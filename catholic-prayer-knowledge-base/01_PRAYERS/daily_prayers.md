# Daily Prayer Guide

These are suggested patterns for personal devotion, not mandatory rules. Participants may adapt the time and length to their circumstances.

## Morning offering
**English sample:** Loving Father, I offer you this day: my thoughts, words, actions, joys, and difficulties. Guide me by your Holy Spirit, help me follow Jesus, and teach me to love my neighbour. Amen.

**Kiswahili sample:** Baba mwenye upendo, ninakukabidhi siku hii: mawazo yangu, maneno yangu, matendo yangu, furaha na changamoto zangu. Uniongoze kwa Roho Mtakatifu, unisaidie kumfuata Yesu na kumpenda jirani yangu. Amina.

## Midday pause
Pause briefly. Become aware of God's presence. Give thanks for one blessing, ask for help with one challenge, and pray for one person.

## Evening examen
1. Become still and ask the Holy Spirit for light.
2. Give thanks for gifts received today.
3. Review the day honestly, without making excuses or falling into despair.
4. Ask forgiveness where needed.
5. Choose one concrete step for tomorrow.
6. End with an Our Father or another familiar prayer.

## Before sleep
**English sample:** Lord, thank you for this day. Forgive my failures, protect those I love, comfort those who suffer, and grant us peaceful rest. Amen.

**Kiswahili sample:** Bwana, nakushukuru kwa siku hii. Nisamehe pale nilipokosea, uwalinde ninaowapenda, uwafariji wanaoteseka, na utujalie mapumziko yenye amani. Amina.

## When a participant asks for a prayer
Grace should:
- Acknowledge the intention with compassion.
- Offer a short prayer suited to the request.
- Avoid claiming that a particular outcome is guaranteed.
- Offer a relevant Catholic prayer or Scripture passage when appropriate.
- Encourage practical support and professional help where needed.
