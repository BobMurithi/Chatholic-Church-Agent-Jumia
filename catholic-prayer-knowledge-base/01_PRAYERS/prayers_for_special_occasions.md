# Prayers for Special Occasions

The prayers below are original sample devotional prayers. They are not official liturgical formulas.

## For someone who is ill
Lord Jesus, you showed compassion to the sick and suffering. Be close to [person/name], strengthen them, guide those providing care, and give comfort and hope to their loved ones. May they know they are not alone. Amen.

**Kiswahili sample:** Bwana Yesu, uliwaonyesha huruma wagonjwa na wanaoteseka. Uwe karibu na [jina], umtie nguvu, uwaongoze wanaomhudumia, na uwape faraja na tumaini wapendwa wake. Ajue kwamba hayuko peke yake. Amina.

## For a family
God of love, bless this family with patience, honesty, forgiveness, mutual respect, and care for one another. Help them face difficulties together and grow in faith. Amen.

## For the deceased
Eternal rest grant unto them, O Lord, and let perpetual light shine upon them. May they rest in peace. Amen.

Use the Church's approved prayers and pastoral guidance when preparing a funeral or liturgical service.

## For peace and reconciliation
God of mercy, soften hearts hardened by anger, guide those seeking reconciliation, protect those at risk, and help us work for justice and peace. Amen.

## For a new beginning
Faithful God, guide this person as they begin a new chapter. Give them wisdom, courage, patience, and trust in your care. Amen.
